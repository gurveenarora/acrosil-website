Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding custom manufacturing, materials, lead times, and catalogue downloads.

1. Can Acrosil manufacture custom rubber and PTFE parts from technical drawings?

Yes! Customized manufacturing is one of our core specialties. We design, mold, and machine rubber, silicone, and PTFE components strictly according to client engineering drawings, 3D CAD models, or physical samples.

2. What temperature ranges can your silicone and PTFE products withstand?

Our silicone components operate comfortably between -60°C and +250°C and are repeatedly autoclavable. Virgin PTFE Teflon parts can handle temperature extremes from -200°C up to +260°C.

3. How can I download the complete product catalogue?

You can download our official Product Catalogue PDF freely by clicking the "View Catalogue" button in the header navigation or footer. No registration or form filling is required to view our catalog.

4. What is the typical lead time for custom tooling and export dispatches?

Standard inventory items (such as standard silicone tubes, TC gaskets, and sifter springs) are dispatched promptly. Custom molded tooling and specialized extrusions are scheduled according to component complexity.
