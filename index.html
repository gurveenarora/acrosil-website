Technical Excellence & Material Integrity

Material Compliance & Engineering Standards

All Acrosil polymer components are manufactured using high-grade compounds tested for temperature stability, chemical resistance, and non-contaminating purity required in sanitary environments.

Food Grade Silicone

High-purity Platinum Cured & Peroxide Cured Silicone elastomers. Odorless, non-toxic, transparent/white, withstands continuous temperatures up to 250°C and repeated autoclave sterilization cycles.

100% Virgin PTFE Teflon

Chemically inert virgin and filled (Glass/Carbon) PTFE Teflon components. Complete resistance to harsh acids, solvents, and extreme cryogenic to elevated temperatures (-200°C to +260°C).

Certified Lead-Free Screens

Precision SS316 and SS304 sifter screen sieves certified 100% Lead-Free. Engineered specifically for cleanroom powder classification and active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) processing.

Specialized Synthetic Rubbers

Custom compounding in Viton (FKM), EPDM, Neoprene, Nitrile (NBR), and Anti-Static compounds tailored for oil resistance, ozone stability, and mechanical durability.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Can Acrosil manufacture custom rubber and PTFE parts from technical drawings?

Yes! Customized manufacturing is one of our core specialties. We design, mold, and machine rubber, silicone, and PTFE components strictly according to client engineering drawings, 3D CAD models, or physical samples.

2. What temperature ranges can your silicone and PTFE products withstand?

Our silicone components operate comfortably between -60°C and +250°C and are repeatedly autoclavable. Virgin PTFE Teflon parts can handle temperature extremes from -200°C up to +260°C.

3. How can I download the complete product catalogue?

You can download our official Product Catalogue PDF freely by clicking the "View Catalogue" button in the header navigation or footer. No registration or form filling is required to view our catalog.

4. What is the typical lead time for custom tooling and export dispatches?

Standard inventory items (such as standard silicone tubes, TC gaskets, and sifter springs) are dispatched within 24-48 hours. Custom molded tooling and specialized extrusions typically ship within 7 to 14 working days depending on complexity.
